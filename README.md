### 🌟 Why Gamify?
Software engineering is a marathon, not a sprint. Turning your profile into a game with visual trackers and achievements is a surprisingly effective way to stay consistent, keep your motivation high through tough topics (looking at you, System Design), and publicly show your dedication to the craft.
